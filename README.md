## Selected Projects

### PCB Design & Embedded Systems

#### [STM32F411 IMU Development Board](https://github.com/Habib-creater/stm32f411-imu-development-board)

4-layer STM32F411CEU6 development board with an MPU-6050 6-axis IMU, USB, SWD, GPIO expansion, power regulation, and I²C communication.

**Tools:** Altium Designer · STM32 · MPU-6050 · I²C · 4-Layer PCB

#### [ESP32 IoT Monitoring & Control Board](https://github.com/Habib-creater/ESP32-IoT-Monitoring-Control-Board)

4-layer ESP32-WROOM-32D board featuring temperature and humidity sensing, USB-C programming, Qwiic I²C, relay control, power regulation, and protection circuitry.

**Tools:** KiCad · ESP32 · Si7021 · I²C · USB-C

#### [MAX30102 Optical Sensor Board](https://github.com/Habib-creater/MAX30102_Optical_Sensor_Board)

2-layer sensor board for optical heart-rate and SpO₂ sensing using the MAX30102, with dedicated sensor power, LED supply, I²C communication, and protection circuitry.

**Tools:** KiCad · MAX30102 · I²C · Sensor Systems

#### [STM32 Smart Power Bank](https://github.com/Habib-creater/STM32F411-Smart-Power-Bank)

STM32F411-based power management system using INA219 for battery current and power monitoring, with an SSD1306 OLED display and USB-C power features.

**Tools:** STM32 · Embedded C · INA219 · OLED

### RF & Antenna Design

#### [1×4 Cylindrical Patch Antenna Array](https://github.com/Habib-creater/1x4-cylindrical-patch-antenna-array)

1×4 microstrip patch antenna array conformally bent onto a 30 cm-radius cylinder and phase-corrected for beam steering from 0–50°. Simulated in CST Studio Suite at approximately 2 GHz.

**Tools:** CST Studio Suite · Microstrip Antenna · Antenna Array · Beam Steering · Electromagnetics

#### [2 GHz Microstrip Patch Antenna](https://github.com/Habib-creater/2ghz-microstrip-patch-antenna)

Inset-fed microstrip patch antenna designed on an FR-4 substrate and simulated in CST Studio Suite, with a resonant frequency near 2 GHz.

**Tools:** CST Studio Suite · Microstrip Antenna · RF Design · Electromagnetics
